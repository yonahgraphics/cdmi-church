Who we are

A church builton prayer.

We are Christian Discipleship Ministries International (CDMI), a church family in Kololo, Kampala. We pray, we study the Word, and we help people become lifelong followers of Jesus.

Vision
To raise a revived and discipled Christian generation with a servant and father heart.
Mission
To help raise and disciple a generation of prayerful Christians by mentoring, training, prayer networking and equipping them with biblical transforming principles and skills.

Our motto is simple. Go into all the world and make disciples. Everything we do, from the prayer altar to the classroom, is about helping one more person know Jesus and follow Him for life.

Discipleship School

From new believerto disciple-maker.

Four classes that take you deeper. Start with Foundations and keep going. You get a mentor along the way, and then you teach others.

Ask how to join
  1. Step 01

    Foundation Classes

    Start here. Salvation, God, the Holy Spirit, prayer, the Bible and the local church.

  2. Step 02

    Discipleship Classes

    Move from knowing the truth to living it and sharing it.

  3. Step 03

    Mentorship Classes

    Get paired with a mature believer who walks with you, one on one.

  4. Step 04

    Baptism Classes

    Learn what baptism means, then take the step in front of your church family.
