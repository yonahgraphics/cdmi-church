Who we are

Raising godly altars of prayer and pursuing revival.

Christian Discipleship Ministries International is a family of believers in Kololo, Kampala, helping individuals, families, churches and communities encounter Jesus and grow into mature disciples.

Under the leadership of Apostle Dr. Michael Kimuli, CDMI is built on three convictions written on our seal: teaching the whole truth, delivering the captives, and making disciples.
